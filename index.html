A proposed partnership helping small and medium avocado farmers in Urrao recover after extreme weather — while protecting their relationships with buyers.

How it works

Support that works on two fronts

The proposal combines practical recovery funding with pre-agreed contract flexibility, so a disruption does not have to end a farmer’s route to market.

🌧️

1. Report damage

Farmers report weather-related damage through their cooperative and share basic details about the impact.

Open report form →
🌱

2. Verify locally

An agronomist or approved independent party checks the damage before eligibility is assessed.

See verification →
🤝

3. Provide recovery support

A proposed package can cover seedlings, essential repairs, technical advice and short-term assistance.

View sample package →
📦

4. Protect market access

Pre-agreed contract adjustments can provide flexibility with deliveries and protect the buyer relationship.

Track recovery →
Prototype note: this is a concept for discussion with farmers, cooperatives, exporters and importers. The programme, eligibility rules, contribution amounts and contract terms have not been established.

How the fund could be governed

A trusted independent administrator could manage the money, with oversight from farmers, the cooperative, exporters, importers and an independent agricultural expert.

Contract and deliveries

A disaster-triggered resilience contract could set out temporary delivery adjustments during verified recovery.

Proposed contract adjustments
  • Flexible delivery dates
  • Temporarily reduced delivery volumes
  • Protection from disruption-related penalties
  • Minimum purchasing commitment during recovery
These are proposed terms for discussion, not an existing or legally binding contract.
